Abstract
The healthcare sector in Pakistan faces persistent operational inefficiencies in managing patient appointments due to heavy reliance on manual telephone-based
 or 
form-based
 scheduling systems. These traditional practices place significant administrative burden on hospital staff, restrict patient access to services outside working hours, and frequently result in scheduling errors and communication delays. To address these challenges, this project proposes HALO, an 
agentic
 conversational appointment management platform designed specifically for hospitals operating in Pakistan.
 
HALO will automate routine appointment-related interactions through conversational artificial intelligence capable of handling bookings, rescheduling, and cancellations without human intervention. 
The system will provide patients with voice-based and text-based interaction modes accessible through hospital website integrations, as well as a dedicated telephony-based interaction channel that allows patients to connect directly to the HALO AI agent via a hospital-provided 
phone number
 and
 will support bilingual communication 
such as
 English and Urdu. Real-time verification of 
doctoral
 availability and automated slot allocation will ensure accurate scheduling while minimizing conflicts and double bookings.
 
The platform will include role-based management tools allowing hospital managers to monitor appointments, manage doctor schedules, and record in-person walk-in appointments when required. A centralized administrative layer will oversee hospital onboarding, subscription management, platform configuration. Unlike marketplace-based healthcare platforms, HALO will operate as a white-label solution, enabling individual hospitals to maintain institutional identity while benefiting from shared infrastructure
. 
By reducing administrative workload, improving appointment accessibility, and minimizing scheduling errors, HALO aims to establish a scalable and efficient digital foundation for appointment management in the Pakistani healthcare system.
Introduction
Hospitals across Pakistan continue to depend largely on manual appointment scheduling methods, primarily handled through telephone calls and physical registers
 by
 human
 receptionists
. These approaches limit patient accessibility, increase administrative workload, and restrict appointment services to specific operating hours. As healthcare demand grows and patient expectations evolve, there is an increasing need for automated systems that can manage appointments efficiently while remaining accessible to diverse patient populations.
 
The 
HALO
 platform is proposed as an 
automated
 conversational appointment management system that hospitals can integrate directly into their existing websites. 
The system will enable patients to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments and obtain basic hospital information through natural conversations conducted via web-based voice or text interaction, or through a dedicated hospital-provided phone number that connects directly to the HALO AI agent
. 
All interaction modes, including web-based and telephony-based access, will operate through a single artificial intelligence core
, ensuring consistent system behavior regardless of the communication method selected by the patient.
 
HALO
 will support bilingual interaction 
such as
 English and Urdu to accommodate Pakistan’s linguistic diversity and reduce communication barriers for patients with varying levels of digital literacy. The platform will maintain structured databases containing doctoral profiles, departmental information, consultation schedules, and hospital operational policies, allowing accurate real-time appointment handling.
 
Hospital managers will be provided with dedicated dashboards to monitor daily appointments, update doctor availability, and record
 
walk-in appointments that occur outside the automated booking flow. An administrative control layer will manage hospital registrations, subscription plans
, and system
 configuration. 
HALO
 will operate as a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform, enabling multiple hospitals to use the system while maintaining isolated data environments. Through automation and continuous availability, the platform aims to modernize appointment management processes and improve healthcare accessibility across Pakistan.
 
The system is designed with cost-aware implementation choices to ensure feasibility for academic evaluation while demonstrating real-world applicability in healthcare environments. Furthermore, the modular architecture of HALO allows future enhancements such as mobile applications, expanded language support, and integration with hospital management systems without disrupting the core appointment workflow.
Problem Statement
Pakistani
 hospitals face significant operational challenges in managing patient appointments due to continued reliance on manual, telephone-based
 or form-based
 scheduling systems. Appointment requests are typically handled by reception staff during working hours
 only
, leading to call congestion, unanswered inquiries, and extended waiting times when multiple patients attempt to book appointments simultaneously. These inefficiencies reduce patient satisfaction and delay access to healthcare services.
 
Receptionists often balance in-person responsibilities alongside telephone scheduling, increasing the likelihood of missed calls, incomplete information exchange, and data entry errors. The absence of automated scheduling tools results in frequent problems such as double-bookings and miscommunication regarding doctor availability, particularly when schedule changes occur unexpectedly.
 
Most hospitals lack after-hours appointment booking facilities, forcing patients to delay care or make unnecessary physical visits for scheduling purposes. Additionally, manual systems do not provide automated reminders, contributing to high appointment no-show rates that disrupt physician schedules and waste hospital resources. The use of fragmented record-keeping methods, including paper registers and disconnected spreadsheets, prevents accurate real-time visibility into appointment availability.
 
Language diversity further complicates appointment management when communication occurs exclusively in English or Urdu, limiting effective access for certain patient groups. Collectively, these challenges increase administrative costs, reduce operational efficiency, and hinder optimal utilization of healthcare resources. An automated, bilingual, and continuously available appointment management system is required to address these limitations and improve healthcare service delivery in Pakistan.
Problem Solution
/Objectives
 
of the
 Proposed System
The
 
HALO
 platform is designed to address the
 
limitations of manual appointment management by introducing an 
agentic
 conversational system that automates routine scheduling tasks while supporting hospital-specific operational workflows. Each participating hospital will be provided with a dedicated interface integrated into its existing website, enabling deployment without extensive technical modifications.
HALO will employ conversational artificial intelligence to guide patients through appointment booking processes, including doctor selection by specialty or name, real-time availability checking, slot recommendation, and booking confirmation. 
The system will support voice-based interaction using speech recognition and synthesis, text-based interaction through a chatbot interface, and telephony-based voice interaction through a hospital-provided phone number connected directly to the HALO AI agent
, with bilingual support.
 
Automated validation mechanisms will ensure that appointment slots are allocated accurately based on up-to-date doctor schedules, preventing double-bookings and scheduling conflicts. Confirmation and reminder notifications will be issued through supported communication channels to reduce missed appointments. Routine appointment handling will be fully automated, while hospital staff will retain the ability to record in-person walk-in appointments through management dashboards when required.
 
Hospital managers will access role-based dashboards providing visibility into daily schedules, appointment status, and doctor availability.
 
A centralized administrative module will manage hospital onboarding, verification,
 and
 subscription plans, ensuring consistent system governance.
 
HALO will function as a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform, allowing multiple hospitals to operate independently while sharing core infrastructure. By automating repetitive administrative processes and extending appointment services beyond conventional working hours, the system will improve operational efficiency, reduce administrative workload, and enhance patient access to healthcare services.

 Bridge the communication gap by providing robust bilingual support (English and Urdu) across both voice and text channels

 Streamline workflows with a centralized dashboard that unifies online bookings and physical walk-in data.
(Scalability):
 Deploy a multi-tenant architecture that enables rapid onboarding of new hospitals with standard web integrations.
(Engagement):
 Reduce appointment "no-shows" via automated, multi-channel reminders (Email,
 In-App
).
(Cost Optimization):
 Reduce administrative overhead, allowing hospitals to reallocate human resources to critical care areas.
(Patient Experience):
 Enhance patient satisfaction through natural conversational interactions
, 
eliminating complex form-filling and navigation barriers.
(Reliability):
 Ensure deterministic, auditable scheduling behavior independent of AI outputs.

The scope of the HALO project covers the design and development of a web-based, artificial intelligence
 
assisted appointment scheduling platform for hospitals operating in Pakistan
. 
The system enables
 hospitals to register, undergo administrative verification, and create a dedicated instance to efficiently manage appointments. It provides a bilingual interface in English and Urdu and operates continuously, including outside regular working hours.

anagers

Patients

or 
initiate interaction by dialing a hospital-provided phone number that connects directly to the HALO AI agent, and book, reschedule, or cancel non-emergency appointments based on real-time doctor availability.

HALO

System Limitations/Constraints
The HALO platform is designed to automate administrative appointment management processes; however, the system operates under certain limitations and constraints that define its functional boundaries.
The
 artificial intelligence agent will not perform medical symptom analysis, disease diagnosis, or clinical decision-making, as the system is limited strictly to administrative appointment scheduling tasks.
The
 system will not handle medical emergencies, urgent care requests, or critical patient situations that require immediate medical intervention or emergency response coordination.
The
 system will support English and Urdu languages exclusively during the initial phase and will not accommodate additional regional or international languages.
Appointment categorization and specialist selection are based on patient-provided information; the system cannot validate medical necessity or assess the clinical appropriateness of requested appointments.
The
 platform will not include native mobile applications for Android or iOS devices and will be accessible through web browsers on desktop and mobile devices.
Data Gathering Approach
The data gathering methodology for the HALO project will employ a 
mixed-methods approach
 to ensure a comprehensive understanding of hospital appointment management workflows and patient interaction requirements within the Pakistani healthcare context.
 
Primary Data Collection
:
Structured interviews
 will be conducted with hospital administrators, department managers, and receptionist staff in major cities to explore appointment handling procedures, communication bottlenecks, and automation needs. 
Questionnaires
 will be distributed to patients to capture their experiences, preferred communication channels, language preferences, accessibility challenges, and expectations from digital systems. 
Observational studies
 will document telephone call patterns, patient-staff interactions, booking procedures, and manual handling times. 
Existing hospital data,
 including appointment records, call logs, and scheduling information, will be analyzed to identify appointment volumes, cancellations, peak demand periods, and scheduling conflicts.
Secondary Data Collection
:
A 
literature review
 of academic publications, industry reports, and case studies will provide insights into healthcare appointment systems, conversational AI, and digital health transformations. 
Focus group
 
discussions
 with diverse patient demographics will explore varying needs and accessibility requirements. 
Consultations
 
with
 healthcare IT 
experts
, AI 
specialists
, and UX 
designers
 will provide technical guidance and interface design principles. 
Hospital operational data
, including doctor availability, department schedules, consultation durations, and patient flow statistics, will be collected to understand realistic scheduling constraints.
Data Analysis & Synthesis
:
All gathered data will be synthesized using thematic analysis to identify recurring patterns, critical pain points, priority requirements, and opportunity areas, ensuring that the HALO system effectively addresses operational challenges and meets user needs across diverse hospital settings in Pakistan.
Project Contribution
The HALO platform moves beyond standard digitization to provide a
n
 
AI-assisted, deterministic appointment 
system. 
Key technical and conceptual contributions include:
Unified Multimodal AI:
 Introduces a synchronized interface where voice and text
 
operate simultaneously, allowing users to switch modes seamlessly without losing context.
Localized Bilingual NLU:
 Implements a specialized Natural Language Understanding model optimized for Urdu and English, specifically handling code-switching common in Pakistan.
Autonomous Slot Negotiation:
 Unlike static forms, the AI agent actively negotiates alternative times when requested slots are unavailable, mimicking a human receptionist.
Zero-Friction "White-Label" Injection:
 utilizing a novel embeddable widget architecture that injects complex AI functionality into legacy hospital websites without backend restructuring.
Hybrid Real-Time Synchronization:
 Bridges physical and digital queues by instantly updating availability when walk-ins are logged, preventing conflicts common in disconnected systems.
Contribution Impact:
 These innovations lower the technical barrier for hospitals and democratize access for patients with low literacy, transforming passive booking forms into an active, intelligent service.
